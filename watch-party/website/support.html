Do we both need Netflix?

Yes. Netflix video is protected, so every participant needs their own Netflix account and must open the same title. Couch synchronizes the playback and adds the call — it never streams or copies video.

How do I start a watch party?

Open a title on Netflix, click the Couch icon, choose Create a party, then Copy invite link and send it to your friends. When they open the link, the same show opens and they join automatically.

The video or audio call won't connect

  • Allow the camera/microphone permission prompt on the Netflix page.
  • If the tiles show but stay black, click anywhere on the page — browsers require a click before they play audio from others.
  • Some strict office/school networks block peer-to-peer media; try a different network.

Playback isn't syncing

Make sure everyone is on the same title (the invite link handles this). Use the ⟳ resync button in the overlay to pull everyone to your exact position after ads or buffering.

Is my data private?

Yes — calls, chat, and sync flow directly between participants (peer-to-peer). We run no servers that collect your data. See our Privacy Policy.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome and Microsoft Edge (Chromium-based browsers).
